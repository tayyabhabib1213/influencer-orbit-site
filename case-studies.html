War story 1

The boxes that never posted: how one rule fixed Esther Formula’s post rate.

Confirm-first since Jan 202470%+ post rate vs 30–50% cold gifting Read the storyClose

The situation

Esther Formula, a Korean skincare and beauty supplement brand, needed creator posts at volume across several markets. Like most gifting programs, the default playbook was to ship product and hope. That model loses half or more of its boxes: only 30% to 50% of gifted creators typically post (HypeAuditor).

What we built

One structural change before anything else: no creator gets product until they confirm. Confirmed addresses go to the warehouse daily, and every shipment is tracked to a post link, not a screenshot.

The result

Product stopped disappearing into silence. The confirmation step is why the post rate climbed to 70%+, against 30% to 50% for cold gifting, and it’s the rule every wave has run on for 35 months.

War story 2

Volume without a hire: 50 to 200 posts a month, with no influencer manager on payroll.

2,483 posts live2,000+ collaborations200+ shipments a month0 hires Read the storyClose

The situation

Hitting 50 to 200 posts a month by hand would take a full-time coordinator (about $61K a year in the US) plus software. It would also put the whole program at risk the day that person left.

What we built

A repeatable monthly operation: creator research, hand-vetting, outreach, confirmation, shipping coordination and follow-up to live, run by our team on a defined system. The brand’s only jobs are approving briefs and shipping.

The result

2,483 creator posts live from 2,000+ creator collaborations, with 200+ coordinated shipments a month, month after month since January 2024. Esther Formula has never hired an in-house influencer manager.

War story 3

From posts to a creator asset: a UGC pipeline and a roster the brand owns.

VIP Creator RosterRepeat seeding + paid collabsMonthly ad-ready UGC Read the storyClose

The situation

Posts that sit in a spreadsheet are wasted twice. They don’t feed the ad account, and the best creators get treated like strangers next month.

What we built

Top performers moved to a VIP roster for repeat seeding and paid collabs, and the strongest posts were flagged as ad-ready UGC every month. The program also narrowed over time from supplements and some cosmetics to a tighter product focus, sharpening which creators fit.

The result

The brand gets a growing roster of proven creators and a monthly supply of ad-ready posts. Every creator contact and content link sits in a tracker the brand owns.
