FAQ

Questions founders ask before booking

How much does it cost?

Plans start from $499 a month. Your exact price depends on the plan and your goals, and we quote it on a 30-minute walkthrough after we’ve seen your brand. Every plan starts with a 60-day pilot, then runs month to month. Prepay three months and save 10%.

How fast do the first creator posts go live?

Outreach starts within the first week, and first creator posts go live within 21 days. Your plan’s monthly post count is guaranteed by day 60, counted from when you ship product.

What are UGC assets with usage rights?

Creator videos you’re allowed to run in your own ads. Every plan includes them, equal to at least 30% of its guaranteed posts. The exact usage terms are written into your proposal before anything starts.

Isn’t seeding just awareness? How do I measure ROI?

At three levels: posts against target (guaranteed), creator code and link sales, and the performance of the posts we turn into ads. All three are in your report.

Your team is in Pakistan. How does that work on US time?

Your live tracker updates in real time, so you never wait on a status email. We reply within one business day over email, Slack or WhatsApp, and it’s written into the engagement.

What do we handle on our side?

Three things: shipping gifted product from your own warehouse, your own Spark Ads spend, and paying creators directly on any paid collabs. We handle everything else.
