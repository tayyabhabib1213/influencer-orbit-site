What is product seeding?

Product seeding is sending your product, free, to hand-picked creators so they post about it in their own words. Done at volume, it builds social proof, TikTok search presence and a steady supply of ad-ready UGC for far less than paid placements. The hard part is operations: finding, vetting, confirming, shipping and following up with every creator.

The Orbit Seeding Engine runs in four steps.

One rule runs through all of them: no creator gets product until they confirm. That single change is why 70%+ of our creators post, against 30% to 50% for cold gifting.

  1. Days 1–5

    Competitor Creator Audit

    We map which creators already post for three of your direct competitors, which hooks get the most views, and which formats win in your category. That becomes your brief and outreach scripts, approved by you. Outreach goes live by day 5.

  2. Days 5–21

    Confirm-first seeding

    We hand-vet creators for audience fit, engagement quality and content match, not follower count. Every creator confirms before your warehouse ships anything. You get confirmed addresses daily.

  3. Day 21 on

    Posts live and tracked

    Posts go live and we chase every one until it shows in your live tracker. You see every creator, shipment and post link in real time.

  4. Day 30 on

    Amplify and report

    Your best posts become Spark Ads, and top performers join your VIP Creator Roster for repeat seeding and paid collabs. Your first monthly report lands on day 30.

Each step removes a reason seeding programs stall: wrong creators, ghosted boxes, and good posts that never become ads.
