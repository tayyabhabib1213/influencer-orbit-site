Questions about the Seeding Engine

Isn’t seeding just awareness? How do I measure ROI?

At three levels: posts against target (guaranteed), creator code and link sales, and Spark Ad performance. All three are in your monthly report.

You have one case study. Isn’t that thin?

It’s one brand for 34 months: 2,000+ collaborations and 50 to 200 posts every month. That proves the operation holds up month after month, which a list of one-off campaigns can’t.

Your team is in Pakistan. How does that work on US time?

Your live tracker updates in real time, so you never wait on a status email. Our response commitment is one business day over email, Slack or WhatsApp, and it’s written into the engagement.

A marketplace charges about $30 a post. Why pay a retainer?

Marketplace creators come from a shared opt-in pool, with no brief built for your brand, no competitor audit, no VIP roster and no Spark Ads pipeline. And you don’t own the relationships. At Growth tier you pay about $25 a post for all of it.

We already have a coordinator. What happens to them?

We import your roster and tracker and absorb the operational load. Their creator relationships stay in place, and volume grows from where it is.

Are your creators only in the US?

No. We serve US brands and source creators wherever your customers are. We’ve run seeding with creators across the US, Southeast Asia, the UK, Australia and the UAE.

How much product do we need?

It depends on your tier and product cost. We size it with you on the walkthrough so you never short customer orders.

Who chooses the creators?

We hand-vet them against the brief you approve. You see every confirmed creator in the tracker before anything ships.

How are posts disclosed?

Every creator is briefed to use FTC-compliant disclosure such as #gifted or #ad, or the platform’s branded-content tools.

What if we want to leave?

After the 60-day pilot it’s month to month. You leave with every contact, content link and the tracker.

This is the wrong fit if

  • Your warehouse can’t ship within three business days of creator confirmations
  • You can’t approve briefs within 48 hours
  • You need a guarantee on views or sales rather than posts
  • You’re outside US skincare, cosmetics or beauty

We’ll tell you on the call, not after you’ve paid.
